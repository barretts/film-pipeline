At an isolated polar station, Kate hears her own voice coming from the radio. It says, "James. Don't open the door." Kate looks up and says, "That was my voice." Three knocks sound from outside. James says, "Someone's out there." He opens the station door to a whiteout. When Kate looks over, James is gone. Alone, she leans into the microphone and sends the warning she heard at the start: "James. Don't open the door."
