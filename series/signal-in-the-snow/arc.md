# Story arc

Episode 1: A signal asks for the outer door to be opened. James takes a walkie to check. After his last transmission, three taps answer Kate.
Ends: Kate stays at the base set. James has the walkie and has passed beyond the window. Episode two opens with Kate going out to look for him.
Episode 2: Kate, now the sole guardian of the post, packs supplies and ventures alone onto the endless white expanse, her breath pluming in the sub-zero air as she follows James’s last known path, discovering that the ice beneath her boots is not frozen water but a shifting, translucent membrane that vibrates in sympathy with the radio waves she carries in her pocket.
Ends: The membrane reveals a submerged structure beneath the ice, and Kate's radio crackles with a voice that is unmistakably James's, telling her to stop moving forward because the signal is no longer coming from the post, but from inside the structure itself.
Episode 3: Pushed by the voice of her missing partner, Kate breaches the translucent ice barrier and descends into a cavernous, subterranean chamber where the air is thick with static and the walls are lined with thousands of dormant receivers, only to find that the signal is not an intrusion but a broadcast originating from a massive, dormant machine buried deep within the polar crust, which suddenly ignites with a blinding white light that consumes the chamber and the operator within.
Ends: The machine has fully activated, broadcasting the signal across the globe, and the final shot holds on the empty radio post back at the surface, where a new pair of operators sits in silence, waiting for the next impossible call.
