# Cast

Recurring:
- kate - Kate: Senior operator who has spent a decade at the post and maintains the station's fragile reality.
- james - James: New recruit with a background in signal analysis who becomes obsessed with the anomaly's origin.

Per episode:
- Episode 1: Drift - Local guide hired to transport Kate and James to the first anomaly coordinate on the ice sheet.
- Episode 2: Vance - Former meteorologist stranded by the storm who reveals the signal matches a lost expedition from 1912.
- Episode 3: Silas - The mysterious figure who appears at the signal's source and claims to be the one who sent it.
