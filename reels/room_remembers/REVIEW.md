# Review - The Room Remembers

Three fictional mystery transmissions were selected over the alternate surreal-story and playful-host directions. Each uses an original female character: anime-inspired, hand-drawn 2D, or stylized 3D. The four rounds are revisions of the same three stories.

The final 30 fps renders were checked at 1080x1920 with complete decode, stereo audio, speech correlation, user-readable Windows permissions, and visible scene changes. Sampled frames from the actual exports were inspected at phone size for crop, caption readability and face obstruction. The audio was measured and locally transcribed, but not auditioned; perceived mix quality and exact phoneme alignment need a listener's review.

Round 02 increased and balanced captions and shortened the silent tails. Round 03 held payoff captions through the narration and introduced an open doorway. Round 04 enlarged the small series label and made the doorway shots consistent. The weakest visual remains the hand-drawn character's mouth, where MuseTalk adds softer realistic shading to flat line art; a Wav2Lip comparison produced a more obvious blur and truncated speech, so MuseTalk was retained. The static cutaway artwork also limits the amount of character motion.

No browser-check warnings were retained in the final round. Music and model terms should be checked for the intended publication; no reel has been uploaded.
